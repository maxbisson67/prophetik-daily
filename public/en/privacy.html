Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This Policy explains how Prophetik collects, uses, and protects your information.

Data we collect

  • Account: mobile phone number, display name (via Firebase Authentication).
  • Content: challenge entries, groups, messages, and data you create.
  • Files: avatar and artwork (Firebase Storage).
  • Diagnostics: anonymized usage events (Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics).
  • Notifications: push token for alerts (Expo/Firebase).

How we use your data

  • Provide app features (groups, challenges, results).
  • Secure accounts and prevent abuse.
  • Improve quality through analytics and diagnostics.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share only with service providers required to run the app (e.g. Google Firebase) under their terms.

Retention and deletion

We retain data while your account is active or as required by law. You may delete your account in the app (My Profile or Settings) or by emailing info@prophetik.ca.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your data.

Security

Technical and organizational measures include Firebase Security Rules, token-based authentication, and related controls.
